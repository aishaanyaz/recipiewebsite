Delicious recipes for managing blood pressure

Managing Blood Pressure Through Diet

These recipes are specially curated to be low in sodium while maintaining great taste. Each recipe is designed to help you maintain healthy blood pressure levels without sacrificing flavor.

Tips for Low-Sodium Cooking

  • Use herbs and spices instead of salt to flavor foods
  • Choose fresh or frozen vegetables over canned options
  • Rinse canned foods to remove excess sodium
  • Cook with fresh ingredients rather than processed foods
  • Use citrus juices to enhance flavors naturally
